Associate Degree for Transfer

Communication Studies AA-T

Build strong speaking, listening, argumentation, and interpersonal communication skills while preparing to transfer as a Communication Studies major.

Why Consider Communication Studies?

Communication Studies examines how people create, interpret, and respond to messages in public, interpersonal, group, cultural, and digital settings.

The AA-T prepares you to transfer to a four-year college or university. It also gives eligible students priority admission to the CSU system, although admission to a specific CSU campus or major is not guaranteed.

What You'll Be Able to Do

  • Communicate effectively with different audiences
  • Analyze how messages influence people and communities
  • Develop stronger arguments and evaluate evidence
  • Adapt communication across interpersonal, professional, cultural, and digital contexts

Career Connection

Communication skills are useful in business, marketing, public relations, media, law, education, journalism, human resources, nonprofit work, and other people-centered careers.

Plan Your Path

The Communication Studies AA-T includes 18 units in the major. A counselor can help you select courses strategically, maximize double-counting with general education, and stay on track for transfer.

1 Complete the Required Core

Complete both courses:

  • COMM C1000 – Public Speaking (3 units)
  • COMM 121 – Interpersonal Communication (3 units)

2 List A: Choose Three

Select three courses:

  • COMM 104 – Argumentation and Debate (3 units)
  • COMM 151 – Small Group Communication (3 units)
  • COMM 122 – Intercultural Communication (3 units)
  • COMM 100 – Introduction to Communication Studies (3 units)
  • COMM 190 – Communication and New Media (3 units)
  • COMM 130 – Introduction to Oral Interpretation of Literature (3 units)
  • COMM 102 – Oral Communication II (3 units)
  • COMM 109 – Social Media Strategies (3 units)

3 List B: Choose Your Direction

Select one course below or another List A course you have not already used.

  • ANTHRO 102 – Human Ways of Life: Cultural Anthropology (3 units)
  • PSYC C1000 – Introduction to Psychology (3 units)
  • SOC 001 – Introduction to Sociology (3 units)
  • ENGLISH 102 – College Reading and Composition II (3 units)
  • ENGL C1001 – Critical Thinking and Writing (3 units)
  • JOURNAL 101 – Collecting and Writing News (3 units)

AA-T Degree Requirements

The Communication Studies major requires 18 units as part of the larger 60-unit Associate Degree for Transfer. Some major courses may also satisfy general education requirements.

Interested? Talk With Counseling

An LAMC counselor can help you choose courses, maximize double-counting, confirm transfer requirements, and create a plan for completing the AA-T.
