Certificate of Achievement

Journalism Certificate

Build practical skills in reporting, writing, editing, digital storytelling, public relations, and multimedia communication.

Why Consider This Certificate?

Today's journalism goes far beyond print. Reporters and media professionals work across websites, social media, photography, video, audio, and other digital platforms.

This certificate brings together journalism, social media, public relations, visual communication, and digital storytelling to prepare you for today's multimedia environment.

What You'll Be Able to Do

  • Research, report, write, and edit accurate stories
  • Evaluate sources, evidence, and potential bias
  • Create multimedia stories for digital audiences
  • Use social media, visual media, and public relations strategies professionally

Career Connection

These skills can support work in journalism, writing, digital media, public relations, and content creation , as well as preparation for further study in journalism or media-related fields.

Plan Your Path

To earn the Journalism Certificate of Achievement, complete all 6 required core courses plus 1 elective for a total of 21 units. The program is designed to be completed across Fall and Spring.

Journalism Certificate Required Core
Course Title Units Suggested Term
JOURNAL 100 Social Values in Mass Communication 3 Fall, Spring, & Summer
JOURNAL 101 Collecting and Writing News 3 Fall & Spring
JOURNAL 43 Principles of Public Relations 3 Fall

MEDIART 801 Digital Storytelling 3 Fall
