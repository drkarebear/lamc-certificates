Support at Los Angeles Mission College

Need Help? Start Here.

College involves more than coursework. If something is making it harder to learn, stay enrolled, or reach your goals, there may be a resource that can help.

Looking for the Official LAMC Student Services Site?

This page can help you figure out where to start. You can also go directly to the official Los Angeles Mission College Student Services website for college information, offices, and services.

Visit Official LAMC Student Services

You Don't Need to Know Which Office You Need

Start with what's going on in your life. More than one situation may apply to you, and you can use more than one resource.

You don't have to wait until a problem becomes a crisis. Support can help with a new problem, an ongoing challenge, or something that suddenly becomes difficult.

Not sure whether you qualify for a program? Ask anyway. You don't need to figure out your eligibility before reaching out.
