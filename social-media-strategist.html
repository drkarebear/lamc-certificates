Certificate of Achievement

Social Media Strategist Certificate

Build practical skills in social media, content creation, and strategy.

Why Consider This Certificate?

Organizations need people who can understand audiences, create useful content, and evaluate what works.

This certificate brings together writing, communication, marketing, journalism, media, and technology to prepare you for that work.

What You'll Be Able to Do

  • Create and adapt content for digital audiences
  • Plan communication around goals, audiences, and platforms
  • Apply social media, marketing, and public relations strategies
  • Evaluate content and use digital tools more strategically

Career Connection

These skills can support entry-level work in social media, content creation, marketing, public relations, and communications , as well as freelance, creative, and entrepreneurial projects.

Plan Your Path

To earn the Social Media Strategist Certificate of Achievement, you must complete all of the courses listed below. Courses are typically offered on the following schedule.
