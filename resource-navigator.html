Guided Student Support

LAMC Student Resource Navigator

Tell the navigator what's getting in the way, and it can help you identify Los Angeles Mission College resources that may fit your situation and decide where to start.

You Don't Need to Know the Name of an Office

You can describe what's happening in your own words. You can share as much or as little as you want.

The navigator may suggest more than one resource if several kinds of support could be useful.

Classes, tutoring, writing, Canvas, or technology
Money, food, housing, or basic needs
Health, accessibility, parenting, or life circumstances
Classes, transfer, careers, or future planning
Community, mentoring, or extra support
Something else—or you're not sure where to start

About This Resource: The LAMC Student Resource Navigator is designed to help students explore possible support resources.
